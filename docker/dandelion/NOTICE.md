This container installs DANDELION 0.1.0 from CRAN. DANDELION is licensed under
GPL-3.0. Its source and license are available from the canonical CRAN package
page at <https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=DANDELION> and the CRAN read-only

and filesystem contract. DANDELION results remain statistical hypotheses and
